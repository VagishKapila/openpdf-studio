Workflow

How It Works

Get documents signed and paid in four simple steps.

1

Upload Your Document

Drag and drop any PDF or document. Our editor lets you make final edits, add fields, and prepare for signing.

2

AI Detects Signature Fields

Our AI automatically identifies where signatures, initials, dates, and names should go. Review and adjust as needed.

3

Sign and Protect

Signers complete all fields in a guided flow. Once signed, the document is automatically encrypted and protected.

4

Collect Payment

Optionally collect payment right after signing. The signer is redirected to a secure checkout — you get paid instantly.
