What It Does

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

OpenPDF Studio packs full PDF editing, AI OCR, e-signatures, and payment collection into a single free app. No upsells. No feature gates on the basics.

📝

Full PDF Editing

Add text, images, shapes, and annotations. Merge, split, compress, rotate, reorder pages. Password-protect and encrypt. Fill and save any PDF form.

Free
🔍

AI OCR — Runs Locally

Scan any paper document and make it fully editable. Supports 100+ languages. Powered by Tesseract.js 5 — runs 100% on your device, zero cloud upload.

Privacy-First
✍️

E-Signatures

Auto-detect signature fields, draw or type your signature, sign all fields in one click. Send to others for signature. Track every signer. Flatten signatures permanently.

No Per-Envelope Fees

Payment Collection

The only PDF tool that collects payment after signing. Set a custom amount, generate a Stripe payment link or QR code, and track payment status per document.

About

Built by a contractor
tired of paying Adobe.

I'm Vagish Kapila — I run Sentio Development Inc, a general contracting company, and Varshyl Inc, where I build software for construction and AI workflows.

OpenPDF Studio started because I was paying $300/year for Adobe Acrobat just to sign and edit change orders. I was also paying for DocuSign separately. And chasing deposits with a third tool. It was absurd.

So I built one app that does all three things — and made it free and open source, because this is a problem everyone has.
